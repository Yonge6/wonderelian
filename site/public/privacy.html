隐私政策

WonderElian · 生效日期：2026 年 8 月 31 日

WonderElian 尊重你的隐私。应用无需注册账号，不投放广告，不进行跨应用追踪，也不会出售个人信息。

我们处理的信息

应用内容主要随安装包提供。语言偏好、阅读位置等设置仅保存在你的设备上。WonderElian 不运营用于收集这些本地数据的用户账户或服务器数据库。

你主动使用的功能

当你主动使用系统分享时，iOS 会按照你的选择把标题或链接交给你选定的应用；WonderElian 不接收分享内容。访问外部作品或网站需要网络连接，并受相应网站自己的隐私政策约束。

Privacy Policy

WonderElian · Effective August 31, 2026

WonderElian respects your privacy. The app requires no account, displays no advertising, does not track you across apps, and does not sell personal information.

Information we handle

Most content is bundled with the app. Preferences such as language and reading position remain on your device. WonderElian does not operate a user-account system or server database that collects these local settings.

Features you choose to use

When you invoke the system share sheet, iOS passes the title or link only to the app you select; WonderElian does not receive the shared content. External projects and websites require a network connection and are governed by their own privacy policies.
