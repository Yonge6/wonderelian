隐私政策

WonderElian · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 2 日

WonderElian 尊重你的隐私。网站无需注册账号，不投放广告，不进行跨网站追踪，也不会出售个人信息。

我们处理的信息

语言、主题和使用统计选择保存在你的浏览器中。WonderElian 不运营用于收集这些本地设置的用户账户系统。

可选使用统计

使用统计默认关闭。你主动开启后，网站或 WonderElian App 使用 Google Analytics 记录受控的内容编号、功能操作结果、前台活跃时长和文章阅读进度，用于改进产品。App 与网站使用独立的事件口径。统计可能使用假名化的浏览器或设备标识，但不上传姓名、联系方式、文章正文、声音内容或你输入的私人内容，也不用于广告或跨网站追踪。你可以随时在页脚关闭；关闭后产品会停止发送新的使用事件并清理可访问的 Google Analytics Cookie。

你主动使用的功能

当你主动使用系统分享时，iOS 会按照你的选择把标题或链接交给你选定的应用；WonderElian 不接收分享内容。访问外部作品或网站需要网络连接，并受相应网站自己的隐私政策约束。

Privacy Policy

WonderElian · Updated October 2, 2026

WonderElian respects your privacy. The website requires no account, displays no advertising, does not track you across websites, and does not sell personal information.

Information we handle

Language, theme and usage-statistics choices remain in your browser. WonderElian does not operate a user-account system that collects these local settings.

Optional usage statistics

Usage statistics are off by default. If you opt in, the website or WonderElian App uses Google Analytics to record controlled content IDs, feature outcomes, foreground active time and article-reading progress so the product can be improved. App events use a dataset separate from website events. Statistics may use pseudonymous browser or device identifiers, but no names, contact details, article text, audio or private input are uploaded, and the data is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking. You can turn statistics off in the footer at any time; the product then stops sending new usage events and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies.

Features you choose to use

When you invoke the system share sheet, iOS passes the title or link only to the app you select; WonderElian does not receive the shared content. External projects and websites require a network connection and are governed by their own privacy policies.
